What currency do they use? How are the people there? What should you do?
The currency is the Czech koruna (CZK), not the euro. It is useful to have some Czech koruna for smaller purchases. People are extremely positive and sweet. Most seem to always be focusing on their selves. You should visit Prague Castle, walk across Charles Bridge, see Old Town Square, and try Czech food like svíčková or trdelník.
